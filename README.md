# Station 9

A first-person horror game you play in your browser.

Station 9 is a research station that's falling apart. The teleport experiment went wrong, and something came through. It can barely see, but it hears everything. Marigold, the station's cheerful voice, will keep you company while you try to get the lights back on and get out alive.

## Play

**[Play Station 9 now](https://nahobbs5.github.io/Station-9/)**. It runs in your browser, so there's nothing to download or install.

Play on a computer with a keyboard and mouse. Headphones are strongly recommended: sound is how you stay alive.

## Your goal

1. Restore main power in the **Generator Room**.
2. Send a rescue call from **Comms**.
3. Prep the **escape pod** and get off the station.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Move |
| Mouse | Look |
| Shift | Sprint (loud, uses stamina) |
| C | Crouch (quiet, slow) |
| E | Use, or hold to repair |
| F | Flashlight |
| Q | Throw a noise-maker · 1–3 choose which kind |
| M | Show or hide the minimap (it only shows places you've seen) |
| T | Stop the audio log that's playing |
| Click / E | Skip to the next line of dialogue |
| Esc | Pause |

## Staying alive

- **Listen.** Slow clicks mean it's patrolling. Quick clicks and a murmur mean it's heard something and is coming to look. When the hum cuts out and the clicking stops, it's about to hunt.
- **Keep quiet.** Sprinting is loud. Crouching is quiet. Holding E to repair is careful; tapping it rapidly is faster but much noisier.
- **Watch your light.** It can only see you when you're moving or lit up, and even the flashlight's click makes a little noise.
- **Use the doors.** Closed doors slow it down and muffle your noise.
- **Find a safe room.** Teal hatches lead to two wellness suites it can never enter. Catch your breath there.

## Noise-makers

Throw one away from you to pull the alien off your trail. There are only seven on the whole station, and you can carry three at a time.

- **Echo Seed** rolls along the floor and chitters three times.
- **Resonance Shard** rings once, and the sound carries a long way.
- **Buzz Pod** hums for 12 seconds. It keeps pulling the alien toward it and covers your footsteps if you stay close.

## The story

Twelve staff terminals around the station hold audio logs that tell what really happened here. They play in your earpiece, so you can keep moving while you listen. Every log you hear adds to your score, and hearing all twelve changes the ending.

## Something isn't right

The alien isn't the only thing on Station 9. Lights fail, doors move on their own, and you may see things that shouldn't be there. The station also hides a few secrets, and achievements are waiting for the curious.

## Settings

Both settings are on the title screen and are remembered between visits.

- **Graphics: High / Low.** High adds reflections, flashlight shadows, bloom, film grain and dust in the air. Choose Low if the game runs slowly on your computer.
- **Apparitions: On / Off.** On is scarier.
